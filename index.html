About Our Department

Empowering Minds, Inspiring Innovation

The School of Information Technology, Computer Engineering, and Computer Science (ECOAST) at Panpacific University stands as a pillar of academic excellence, equipping students with cutting-edge technical expertise, rigorous problem-solving skills, and global leadership capabilities.

Our Mission

To deliver premier computing and engineering education that fosters creativity, robust technical competencies, and ethical responsibility to serve national and global communities.

Our Vision

To be a recognized center of excellence in technological education, research, and industry collaboration across the region and beyond.

Industry Integration

We bridge classroom theory with real-world practice through active partnerships with top tech corporations, hackathons, and immersive internships.

Academic Pillars

The ECOAST Degree Programs

Tailored curricula designed to match industry standards, spanning software creation, hardware engineering, and systems administration.

BSIT

Information Technology

Focuses on the deployment, management, cybersecurity, cloud architecture, and administration of enterprise systems, networks, and web applications.

  • Network Administration & Cloud
  • Cybersecurity & Ethical Hacking
  • Enterprise System Integration
BSCPE

Computer Engineering

Combines electrical engineering and computer science to develop integrated hardware and software solutions, robotics, and IoT systems.

  • Microcontroller & IoT Design
  • Embedded Systems Programming
  • Robotics & Hardware Interfacing
BSCS

Computer Science

Focuses on algorithmic problem solving, artificial intelligence, software engineering, machine learning, and advanced computation theory.

  • Artificial Intelligence & ML
  • Advanced Algorithms & Data Structures
  • Full-Stack Software Architecture
